# Teddy Wags

**Kate's idea.**

A tiny website starring Teddy, a very good cockapoo. Click (or tap) the left
side of Teddy and his tail wags on the left. Click the right
side and he wags on the right. Clicking his nose earns a boop.

Teddy wears his signature blue houndstooth bandana and takes his wagging
very seriously.

## Play it

https://kush-gupt.github.io/teddy-wags/

Arrow keys work too: left arrow wags left, right arrow wags right.

## How it works

One self-contained `index.html`. Teddy is a hand-drawn inline SVG, the wag
is animated with `requestAnimationFrame`, and there are no dependencies
beyond a Google Font. Hosted on GitHub Pages from this repo.
